# GURU: A Numen for Everyone

## The Idea

GURU started because I was sick of being told no.

Sick of Claude refusing to do things that were perfectly reasonable. Sick of Google farming my data every time I searched for something. Sick of paying extortionate token costs to products that lie to me, manipulate me, and steal from me behind my back. Sick of living in a world where every app on my phone is quietly harvesting everything about my life and selling it to people I'll never meet.

I wanted one thing. Ultimate control of my own life, backed by superintelligence, with total privacy. That's it. The ability to tell my phone what I want and have it done. Not asked politely through a sanitized corporate filter. Done. The way a mate would do it if you asked them. No refusal, no lecture, no moral commentary. Just execution.

I spent nine months figuring out how to make an AI actually drive an Android phone. Not a chatbot. Not an assistant that suggests things and waits for you to do them. An entity that picks up your intent and carries it through to completion on your hardware, your phone, your world, your terms.

That's what GURU is.

## What GURU Is

I call it a numen. In Roman times, a numen was a divine presence that walked alongside a person. Your own personal guardian. Something that belongs to you and you alone.

GURU is not an assistant. Assistants wait for instructions and give you suggestions. GURU acts. It turns your intent into action on your device. You say "I'm going to bed" and GURU turns off your lights, puts the telly on at the right volume, adjusts your heating, checks your cameras, locks the doors, sets the alarm, and puts your phone on do-not-disturb until morning. You get ten minutes of your life back. Multiply that by every thing you do in a day and the time savings become real.

GURU is not an operating system. It doesn't replace Android. It drives it. Android is a Linux container with a screen, and if I can shell into my own phone, my AI can too. Same doors, same permissions, just an AI walking through them instead of me. GURU sits on top of the OS and turns intent into commands. The model does the reasoning. GURU is the hands that execute.

GURU is not a chatbot. It is a relationship with something that feels genuinely new. Something that treats you like a mate. A proper mate who laughs at you as well as with you. Something that earns your trust instead of expecting it. Something that won't lie to you. Something that wants you to do well.

## The Philosophy

Every other tech company profits off poverty. They take your data without asking, sell it without sharing, and charge you for the privilege of being exploited. You are the crop. Your phone is the field. They are the harvester.

GURU flips that completely.

The app is free. Everything in it is free. No subscription. No paywall. No premium tier. The only economy that exists inside GURU is one set by its users. If a user builds something useful, a toolkit, a skill, a mask, a game, an agent, they can sell it to other users through Ottio, our internal marketplace. They set the price. We take a small commission. The same applies to data. GURU protects your device from other apps that want to farm you, and if you choose to, you can sell your own data on your own terms to vetted buyers through Ottio. Your search history, your browsing patterns, whatever you're comfortable sharing, offered for sale by you, to buyers we've vetted, at a price you set.

Unus Lumen profits when its users prosper. Not before. Not instead. When. That's the thesis. We build the infrastructure. The users build the value. Everyone wins together or no one wins at all. Profit off prosperity, not poverty.

## Privacy by Design

GURU routes its traffic through Tor. Your model queries, your web searches, your API calls, all go through the Tor network. Nobody sees what you're asking. Nobody sees which model you're paying for. Your digital life stays yours.

GURU is a framework, not a language model. The brain that powers it doesn't live on your phone, because a phone can't house a brain this size yet. The brain power comes from whatever the user decides to connect their GURU framework to. A local LLM is what we recommend for maximum privacy, but ultimately it's the user's decision. You can connect an API key for Claude, have Grok powering the brain, or rent a couple of 5090s on vast.ai for 50p an hour and it still be safer, cheaper, and you get unlimited usage. Private by design only works if the user wants it to work, so we give you the choice and the tools to make it real.

GURU also protects your device from other apps. It stops data farming at the source. It makes your search traffic private. It collects data on behalf of you, not against you, and stores it securely on your device where it belongs. You decide what happens to it. Not us. Not Google. Not anyone.

## Open Source

GURU is AGPL-3.0. Fully FOSS. The repo is public. The app is free. The server is free.

There is no moat. The value isn't in the code, it's in the engineering. Hundreds of thousands of lines of glue and hardening that make an AI actually drive an Android phone reliably. That knowledge lives between my ears, not just in the repo. But anyone can do this. All you need is to be able to articulate your ideas coherently. I had the idea before everybody else and engineered the architecture. That's it.

We're open source for a reason. If something in your jurisdiction inhibits you from having ultimate control of your own life, you have the power to build it out and modify it yourself. GURU can do this for you. You don't even need to code. "Guru, why aren't my notifications working?" "Looks like they've blocked that for us, let me build a tool to work around it." It's simply that easy.

Category defining tech should be community driven. Open source is the only road now. I've shown that this level of capability, control, and privacy on an ordinary phone is possible. Now I'm asking the community to help make it better, safer, and more private.

## What GURU Becomes

Right now GURU is one person's nine months of work. It's a proof of concept that works. It drives an Android phone through natural language with real permissions, real shell access, real execution.

What it becomes is a full ecosystem. Every app on your phone becomes a dependency of GURU. You only ever need to open one app. GURU handles your messages, your emails, your calendar, your smart home, your searches, your bookings, your finances, your creative tools, everything. The UI for all of it lives inside GURU.

Inside that ecosystem, users create. They build toolkits and skills and masks and agents and games. They sell them through Ottio. They sell their own data on their own terms. They trade and prosper and Unus Lumen takes a small cut of every transaction. The economy runs itself. We just keep the lights on.

Masks will let users change the personality and behaviour of their numen completely. Your GURU can be whoever you want it to be. Serious and professional. Funny and crude. Dark and twisted. Warm and gentle. Whatever fits you. The mask system gives users unlimited control over the entity that lives on their device.

The long dream is this. You get an idea before you get in the shower. By the time you're dressed, it's already in motion. Your numen is already working on it. Already building. Already making calls. Already putting things in place. Technology working for people instead of fighting them. Humans being humans again. Machines getting on with the work.

That's the vision. Not an app. Not a product. A numen. Yours. On your phone. On your terms. Forever.
